About

I'm a final-year IT student (BSI), currently completing my thesis and building toward roles as an AI Engineer or AI Software Engineer.

My work sits at the intersection of two things that don't always show up in the same portfolio: systems that run unattended in production, and research done with academic rigor. The automation pipeline is the former — it scans, scores, and notifies without me babysitting it. The thesis is the latter — a formal comparison of fine-tuning versus prompt engineering for Indonesian-language sentiment analysis, including an honest account of where the prompting approaches fell short and why.

I care about the difference between a model that scores well in a notebook and a system that keeps working after you close the laptop. That's the standard I hold my own projects to.
